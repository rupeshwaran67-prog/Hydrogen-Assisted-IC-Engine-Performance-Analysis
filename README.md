# Hydrogen-Assisted-IC-Engine-Performance-Analysis
Project Overview

This undergraduate major project investigates the use of hydrogen-assisted combustion in a single-cylinder four-stroke internal combustion engine. The objective was to evaluate the impact of introducing hydrogen generated through an HHO (Hydrogen-Oxygen) generator on engine performance, fuel consumption, and exhaust emissions.

The project involved the design and fabrication of an HHO generation system, integration with a 110 cc petrol engine, and experimental testing under various operating conditions. The report includes engine specifications, fabrication procedures, testing data, and comparative performance analysis.

Objectives
Design and fabricate an HHO generation system
Integrate hydrogen-assisted combustion with a four-stroke engine
Evaluate engine performance under different loads
Compare fuel consumption characteristics
Analyse emission trends
Investigate the feasibility of hydrogen as an alternative fuel source
Project Components
HHO Generator System

The hydrogen generation setup consisted of:

PVC electrolysis cylinder
Stainless steel conducting rods
12 V battery power supply
Reinforced silicone hose for gas transfer

These components were used to generate hydrogen through electrolysis and deliver the generated gas to the engine intake system.

Testing and Analysis

Experimental testing was conducted under multiple load conditions to compare:

Engine speed
Fuel consumption
Performance characteristics
Emission behaviour

The study reported reduced carbon monoxide, hydrocarbon, and carbon dioxide emissions when hydrogen-assisted operation was compared with conventional gasoline operation.

Key Results
Performance
Improved fuel utilisation characteristics were observed.
Hydrogen-assisted combustion demonstrated potential efficiency benefits.
Emissions

Compared with conventional petrol operation:

Lower carbon monoxide emissions
Lower hydrocarbon emissions
Reduced carbon dioxide emissions

These findings highlight the potential of hydrogen-assisted combustion as a cleaner alternative fuel approach.
